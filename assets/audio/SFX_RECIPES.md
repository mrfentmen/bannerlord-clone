# SFX Recipes

How every sound effect in the game is made. All cues are procedural
numpy synthesis, deterministic from a seed: re-running the generator with
the same seed reproduces the file bit-for-bit. Everything is original,
CC0, no samples, no licences to clear.

- Packs 1-3 (105 cues): generated by `content/audio/sfx/gen_sfx.py`.
  The script is the recipe; families are summarized below.
- Pack 4 (11 cues): generated by `tools/sfx/synth.py`, fully specified
  here. `CUE_SPECS` in that file is the machine-readable source of truth.

All files: 44.1 kHz, 16-bit, mono WAV. Loops are exactly periodic (see
"Seamless loop construction"). All cues normalized toward -16 LUFS
integrated (ITU-R BS.1770-4); per-file loudness in
`content/audio/sfx/LOUDNESS.md`.

## Pack 4 recipes

### Melee impacts

**melee-impact-flesh** (seed 201, 0.28 s) - punch landing on a body.
Low sine thump at 72 Hz with 45 ms exponential decay, plus a soft noise
burst lowpassed at 900 Hz with 12 ms decay. Thump x1.1, noise x0.45.

**melee-impact-armor** (seed 202, 0.45 s) - weapon striking plate armor.
Four inharmonic partials of 392 Hz (ratios 1, 2.44, 3.91, 5.72) with
100-160 ms decays, plus a hard transient: white noise with 8 ms decay
bandpassed 1.8-7 kHz. Ring x0.7, transient x0.55.

### Shield blocks

**shield-block-wood** (seed 203, 0.38 s) - blow absorbed by a wooden
shield. Woody knock: 150 Hz sine, 55 ms decay. Crackle: sparse random
impulses (1.4% density) bandpassed 1.2-4.2 kHz, 60 ms decay. Low thud:
62 Hz sine, 70 ms decay. Knock x0.8, crackle x0.9, thud x0.7.

**shield-block-metal** (seed 204, 0.55 s) - blow glancing off a metal
shield. Bright inharmonic clang: partials of 311 Hz at ratios 1, 2.71,
5.13, 7.96 with 160-280 ms decays, plus a scrape: white noise, 20 ms
decay, bandpassed 3.2-9 kHz. Ring x0.85, scrape x0.35.

### Vehicle engine loops

One shared builder (`engine_loop`). A low harmonic stack (body frequency
plus 2nd/3rd harmonics at 0.5/0.25 weight) amplitude-chopped at the chug
rate, over two periodic noise beds: brown noise lowpassed at `noise_lp`
(exhaust) and brown noise bandpassed at `rumble_bp` (mechanical rumble).
The chop depth sets how hard the idle "lopes". Variants:

| Cue | Seed | Chug | Body | Depth | Exhaust LP | Rumble BP |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| tank-engine-idle | 205 | 6.5 Hz | 42 Hz | 0.60 | 360 Hz | 150-600 Hz |
| tank-engine-rev | 206 | 13 Hz | 58 Hz | 0.45 | 520 Hz | 220-900 Hz |
| truck-engine-idle | 207 | 8 Hz | 55 Hz | 0.60 | 480 Hz | 200-750 Hz |
| truck-engine-rev | 208 | 15 Hz | 72 Hz | 0.45 | 640 Hz | 260-1000 Hz |

Idle variants chop deeper and filter darker; rev variants run twice the
chug rate with brighter filters. The existing `tank-engine` /
`truck-engine` cruise loops remain the mid-state; the client blends
idle/cruise/rev by throttle.

### Crowd battle layers

One shared builder (`crowd_battle_loop`, 6.0 s each). A low brown-noise
bed (lowpassed 260 Hz) plus N voice-like shouts: bandpassed periodic
noise (formant band randomized 500-1100 Hz to 1.6-3.6 kHz per voice)
with two integer-cycle amplitude LFOs, plus M distant impact booms
(0.9 s lowpassed burst + 42 Hz thump) placed circularly. The client
crossfades the three layers by live battle intensity.

| Cue | Seed | Voices | Shout gain | Impacts | Impact gain | Bed gain |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| crowd-battle-low-loop | 209 | 8 | 0.7 | 3 | 0.5 | 0.25 |
| crowd-battle-mid-loop | 210 | 16 | 1.0 | 6 | 0.7 | 0.32 |
| crowd-battle-high-loop | 211 | 28 | 1.3 | 10 | 0.9 | 0.40 |

## Seamless loop construction

Pack-4 loops are exactly periodic over their duration: every tonal
component completes an integer number of cycles (asserted at build time;
a non-integer parameter fails loudly instead of shipping a click), and
all noise is synthesized in the frequency domain (random phases at the
DFT bins, shaped magnitudes), which is periodic by construction. No
crossfade is applied. Impact booms are placed circularly so their decay
wraps around the boundary.

Eight pack-1/2/3 loops shipped with single-sample boundary clicks from
an older crossfade helper; they were repaired in place with a C1 Hermite
spline over ~6 ms at the boundary (`tools/sfx/repair.py`, one-time).
34/34 loops now verify seamless.

## Packs 1-3 recipe families

Full detail is in `content/audio/sfx/gen_sfx.py` (deterministic, seeded).
Families:

- **Weapons by tier**: filtered noise bursts (bandpass per weapon body)
  plus sine "crack" transients; tiered by brightness and decay.
- **Vehicle engines** (motorcycle/jeep/truck/armored/tank): harmonic
  stack plus lowpassed noise, same architecture as pack 4.
- **Ambience beds** (wind, rain, tavern, blacksmith, campfire, town):
  shaped noise through lowpass/bandpass with slow amplitude LFOs.
- **Battle** (drums, horns, arrows, catapults, siege): modal
  percussion (decaying sine partials) and noise whooshes.
- **UI / campaign / music stingers**: short sine blips and two-note
  motifs with exponential decays.

Per-cue trigger, category, loop flag, and licence: `content/audio/sfx/`
`SFX.md` (pack 1), `SFX2.md` (pack 2), `SFX3.md` (pack 3), `SFX4.md`
(pack 4), and the machine-readable `audio-manifest.json`.
