# Combat Barks And Battlefield Dialogue

Short lines shouted over a firefight. Troops, scouts, gunners, medics, captains,
and the player. These are the lines that play in the middle of a battle, so
every one of them has to be short enough to yell over an engine or a rifle and
still be understood.

Rules for anything added here later: plain punctuation only, no em dashes, no
fancy quotes, one thought per line, no delivery notes or stage directions in the
line itself. Keep it to one sentence where you can and never more than two.
Nobody shouts in complete paragraphs. A bark that takes four seconds to finish
is a bark that gets heard after the thing it was warning about has already
happened.

Groups are ordered the way the game can pull them. Unit callouts first, since
those are about what a soldier sees. Morale by situation, because a man who is
winning and a man who is breaking sound nothing alike. Commander orders after
that, since a captain talking over his own troops is the loudest thing on the
field. Kill shouts, victory shouts, and the companion lines last, because those
are what the player remembers.

Labels are speaker classes the game can pick from. A line is never attached to
a specific named character. Companions speak through their own class label so
that any companion can carry any line.

- **Troop:** They are breaking and I can see where it starts.
- **Troop:** Hold the line and we win. Hold it, that is all I am asking.
- **Troop:** Four down from ours and a lot more than that from theirs.
- **Troop:** Keep the pace up, keep the pace up, we are rolling them.
- **Troop:** They have stopped coming. Did everybody notice they stopped coming.
- **Troop:** Breathe. Everybody breathe. We are going to win this.
- **Troop:** Our captain is still on his feet, so I am still on my feet.

- **Troop:** Stay down, stay down, the rounds are going over.
- **Troop:** Do not stand up for anything except the captain's word.
- **Troop:** Pick up the rifle off the body, that is an order, not a suggestion.
- **Troop:** It is not bullets up there, it is pieces of road. Stay down anyway.

## Commander Orders

Short battlefield commands. The player shouts these and the AI captains shout
the same words, so every line has to work as both an order and a thing a person
would yell. Most are single sentences. A few are two.

### Movement

- **Captain:** Forward, all of you, keep it up.
- **Captain:** Hold where you are, nobody crosses that line.
- **Captain:** Left flank, swing left, go now.
- **Captain:** Right flank, take the open ground, do not wait for me.
- **Captain:** Fall back twenty yards, twenty yards and no more.
- **Captain:** Close up on me, I want the line shut.
- **Captain:** Double time, we are behind the schedule.
- **Captain:** Stop where you are and let them come to us.
- **Captain:** Do not cross the road until I give the word.
- **Captain:** Form on the ridge and hold the high side of it.

### Fire

- **Captain:** Hold your fire. Hold it until I count.
- **Captain:** Fire, all guns, center mass.
- **Captain:** Cease fire, cease fire, they are not there anymore.
- **Captain:** Shift left, thirty yards, everyone shift left.
- **Captain:** Rounds on that gun, ignore the trucks for one minute.
- **Captain:** Save your ammunition, do not shoot at smoke.
- **Captain:** Shoot at the ones standing. Shoot at the ones standing.
- **Captain:** Nobody fires past the ditch. That is the whole rule.

### Reserves And Support

- **Captain:** Reserves, forward, on my left.
- **Captain:** Reserve stays back until I say, that is what a reserve is for.
- **Captain:** Gunners, walk your guns up to the fence line.
- **Captain:** Medics, get to the man in the ditch and forget the fight.
- **Captain:** Supply, we need ammunition in the next ten minutes or we lose this.
- **Captain:** Scouts, find me a way around them and do not announce yourself.
- **Captain:** Water first, then food, in that order, and do not argue.

### Under Pressure

- **Captain:** Hold the line. Whatever else happens, the line holds.
- **Captain:** Withdraw to the road, and do it in order, and do it fast.
- **Captain:** Everybody back twenty yards, I am doing the same thing.
- **Captain:** They want us to break here. We are not breaking here.
- **Captain:** We are outnumbered and that changes nothing until somebody panics.
- **Captain:** Pick your ground, do not let them pick it for you.
- **Captain:** Steady, steady. Slow is fine. Fast and dead is not fine.
- **Captain:** I need a body in that ditch and I need one now.

## Kill Shouts, Victory Shouts, And The Hard Ones

### Kills

- **Troop:** Got one.
- **Troop:** Down, he is down.
- **Troop:** That is two of them and nobody helped me.
- **Troop:** I hit him at thirty feet and he did not get a word out.
- **Troop:** That one had a radio. Somebody get that radio.
- **Troop:** He had a pistol and he drew it, and now he does not have it.
- **Troop:** That was close. Too close. I can still feel it.
- **Troop:** Three on that rock and I put all three on it.
- **Troop:** Do not crowd the body, check him, then do not stand there.
- **Troop:** I put him down and then I stood there a second, and that second cost me.

### Victory

- **Troop:** That is the last of them, that is the last of them right there.
- **Troop:** They broke. They broke and they are not coming back.
- **Troop:** We won. We actually won this one.
- **Troop:** Nobody cheer until I check the tree line, nobody.
- **Troop:** Give them a minute. Let them look at the field first.
- **Troop:** Put your weapons down slow. Nobody gets hurt now, so slow.
- **Troop:** Get the wounded out before we do anything else, including this.
- **Troop:** Somebody start counting what we can carry off this road.
- **Troop:** It is quiet. I do not like how quiet it is.

### A Companion Goes Down

These are the lines the game plays when a companion is killed in battle. Keep
them short. Nobody has breath for a speech. The speaker is a surviving
companion, not a narrator.

- **Companion:** I am hit. I am hit, I am down here.
- **Companion:** Get off me, go, get out from under me.
- **Companion:** Take the book out of my pocket. That is all I want.
- **Companion:** Tell them I said the road, not the town. Road.
- **Companion:** I was going to tell you later. I am telling you now.
- **Companion:** Do not stand over me. Go help somebody who can still walk.
- **Companion:** You were right about all of it. Say that at the table.
- **Companion:** It is all right. It is all right. Keep moving.
- **Companion:** I have got the good coffee in the truck. Somebody drink it.
- **Companion:** Finish the job. Do not come back for me, finish the job.
- **Companion:** My people are in the north. Somebody tell them it was the road.
- **Companion:** Give my rifle to the next man who asks for it.
- **Companion:** This was a good crew. Tell somebody that too.
- **Companion:** I got them out. I want that said out loud before I stop.
- **Companion:** Do not waste the ride back. Take the truck.
- **Companion:** I am not angry. I want you to know I am not angry.
