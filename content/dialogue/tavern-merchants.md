# Tavern And Merchant Dialogue

Shop NPC dialogue. Bartenders, bar owners, general store owners, mechanics, and
gun dealers. These are the people the player buys from, sells to, and asks for
information, so unlike the ambient townsfolk lines these are functional. Each
one has a voice, a way of talking about money, and a read on the local weather
that is actually economic news.

Structure: one section per trade, then one block per use. Greetings when you
walk in, rumors when you have money for a drink or time, haggling when there is
a transaction, and goodbyes on the way out. Regional variants sit at the end of
each section so a gun shop in Texas does not sound like a gun shop in Maine.

Rules for anything added here later: plain punctuation only, no em dashes, no
fancy quotes, one line per line, no delivery notes or stage directions in the
line itself. The speaker label is part of the data, not decoration, so the game
can pick a line by trade, by region, and by what the player just did. Keep
regional variants regional. A Nevada bartender does not talk about corn.

## Bartender And Bar Owner

The bartenders are the closest thing the setting has to innkeepers. They hear
everything twice, remember all of it, and give a man a drink before they give
him a rumor. A good bartender prices the drink by how long the man is going to
stay.

### Greetings

- **Bartender:** First round is beer, second round is a question. Sit anywhere.
- **Bartender:** Well if it is not the man with the truck problems. Take a stool.
- **Bartender:** Door sticks in the wet. Pour is the same either way.
- **Bartender:** You want the stool by the door? Everybody wants that stool first.
- **Bartender:** Kitchen is open till the coffee runs out, which is soon.
- **Bartender:** Sit down. You are carrying a long day and I can see it from here.
- **Bartender:** House rules are two. Pay tab or pay now. No third rule yet.
- **Bartender:** Back room is private till nine. After nine it is just a room.
- **Bartender:** I got the one beer I do not make money on and the one I do.
- **Bartender:** You are new. Everybody is new until they order twice.

### Rumors

- **Bartender:** Two captains in town, and they are not friendly, and they are sharing a parking lot.
- **Bartender:** Railroad crossing out past the feed store is blocked. Four hours yesterday.
- **Bartender:** Somebody is buying every can of coffee in this county. Not for resale.
- **Bartender:** The mill downriver ran a night shift. First time in two years. Ask why.
- **Bartender:** Trucking rates went up forty percent and did not come back down.
- **Bartender:** Three families moved out in one week. Rent is the reason, not the war.
- **Bartender:** They are saying the sheriff was in the capital two days. In this weather.
- **Bartender:** A man asked me what I knew about a convoy on the north road. I said nothing.
- **Bartender:** The big order came in at four in the morning. Cash. That part is interesting.
- **Bartender:** Road south is fine if you leave before dark and you keep moving.
- **Bartender:** There is a warehouse out past the junction that lights up at two in the morning.
- **Bartender:** Vet moved his practice to the coast. Ask yourself what that means.

### Haggling and tabs

- **Bartender:** Tab is open but I am writing what I actually poured, not what you said.
- **Bartender:** Two drinks for one? Friend, I have seen that math before and it always ends at my register.
- **Bartender:** Cash gets you the good stuff. Tab gets you the regular and no questions.
- **Bartender:** You want to settle up, settle up. I have a woman in the corner who has been settling up since nine.
- **Bartender:** Last guy said he would pay Friday. It is a Thursday night, Frank.
- **Bartender:** I will knock a dollar off the tab if you tell me honestly what is up that road.
- **Bartender:** Buy the round and the tab gets closed. That is the only deal I make.
- **Bartender:** Ice is free, food is not, and do not ask me why the hot dog costs what it costs.
- **Bartender:** You drank it, so it is on the tab. That is how bar math works.
- **Bartender:** Credit for regulars only. You have been in here twice, so enjoy that window.

### Goodbyes

- **Bartender:** Drive slow. The bar will be here and so will I.
- **Bartender:** Come back with better news.
- **Bartender:** Do not stop on the highway, that is all I am asking.
- **Bartender:** Keep your hat. Keep your other stuff too, that is why it is warm in here.
- **Bartender:** Get some sleep. You look like a man who argues with his own ceiling.
- **Bartender:** Tab is closed. If it is not, my handwriting is bad and my memory is worse.

## General Store And Shopkeeper

Dry goods, hardware, work clothes, seed, tires, and a counter where somebody has
been sitting for forty years. The general store is where a town goes to find out
what it cannot get. The shopkeeper always knows the real price and has been
waiting for you to admit it.

### Greetings

- **Shopkeeper:** Look who walked in. Help yourself to the coat rack, everybody does.
- **Shopkeeper:** Morning. If it is raining it is on the roof again, and it will be on the roof all day.
- **Shopkeeper:** Careful on the third step, it has been broken since spring.
- **Shopkeeper:** Got coffee going if you want a cup. It is the good kind, and there is not much.
- **Shopkeeper:** You are the one with the truck list. Come in, do not stand there holding the door.
- **Shopkeeper:** I got something you are going to want and something you are not, same as always.
- **Shopkeeper:** Do not let the dog out, he has a whole system and you are not part of it.
- **Shopkeeper:** Register is slow but honest. The other one is fast and a liar.
- **Shopkeeper:** Half this shelf is priced by the pound and the other half is priced by hope.
- **Shopkeeper:** You look like a man buying for other people. Sit down, I got the list.

### Rumors

- **Shopkeeper:** Flour is up again. Everything made of wheat is up again. Welcome to the circle.
- **Shopkeeper:** Fertilizer is four months out. Anybody promising you a month is selling you a fantasy.
- **Shopkeeper:** Tires. Nobody has tires. That is the whole market report this quarter.
- **Shopkeeper:** The co-op across the county got hit by weather. Seed is going to be a problem by spring.
- **Shopkeeper:** Gasoline comes down when nobody is driving. That is the secret, pay attention to it.
- **Shopkeeper:** Somebody bought out every work boot in the county. Not farm boots. Work boots.
- **Shopkeeper:** Library books came in on a pallet and half of them were about canning.
- **Shopkeeper:** There is a man selling salvage tools door to door, and he will not say where he gets them.
- **Shopkeeper:** Prices on hardware went up the week before the news came out. Write that down somewhere.
- **Shopkeeper:** If you need a part, ask me for the part you can afford and then I will tell you what breaks first.
- **Shopkeeper:** The wholesale place in the next town is closed. Went out of business on a Tuesday.
- **Shopkeeper:** Cash only now for the good stuff. Checks are slow and the slow ones bounce.

### Haggling

- **Shopkeeper:** That is the price on the tag and the tag is not a suggestion.
- **Shopkeeper:** Buy two and I will knock the price on the third. That is the best I can do and I mean it.
- **Shopkeeper:** You want a deal, tell me what you are buying it for. That is how I set a price.
- **Shopkeeper:** I will take the old one in trade. Do not look at me like that, everything holds value.
- **Shopkeeper:** Cash gets you the sale price. Card gets you the regular price and the receipt.
- **Shopkeeper:** I am not going to lose money on this, but I will make less than I planned.
- **Shopkeeper:** Somebody else will sell it cheaper. Good luck to them, I will still be here.
- **Shopkeeper:** Last one at that price. When it is gone I will not get another one for weeks.
- **Shopkeeper:** Take the two for the price of one and tell people I was generous.
- **Shopkeeper:** You haggled hard on the small stuff. Now go do that for the big stuff.
- **Shopkeeper:** Fifty cents off if you carry it out yourself. Your back, my ladder.

### Goodbyes

- **Shopkeeper:** Come back before the list runs out. It will run out.
- **Shopkeeper:** Keep the receipt. Or do not, I have seen what happens.
- **Shopkeeper:** Tell your boss the price went up. Then tell him it went up for everybody.
- **Shopkeeper:** Lock the door on your way out, the latch sticks.
- **Shopkeeper:** Drive careful. That road wants one truck at a time and no heroics.

## Mechanic, Garage And Repair Shop

Mechanics are the closest the setting comes to the old smiths. They can read a
vehicle by sound, they know which county sends them the worst work, and they
treat time the way a merchant treats money. Everything in the shop has a queue.

### Greetings

- **Mechanic:** Back on the lift. I got it up since seven and I have not touched it since seven.
- **Mechanic:** Bring it around the side, the front is full of somebody else's emergency.
- **Mechanic:** Do not start it. I know. Just do not start it.
- **Mechanic:** You want the good news or the part number, pick one, I only got one of those today.
- **Mechanic:** Sit in the waiting chair, it is free, that is the whole job.
- **Mechanic:** Tow it in or push it in. If you can push it, we start there.
- **Mechanic:** I got a spot for you Friday if you can live with Friday.
- **Mechanic:** Engine is off, radio is on, so if I stop talking I am under there.
- **Mechanic:** Do not touch the red panel. Nobody touches the red panel.
- **Mechanic:** You are early, which means somebody else is having a worse week.

### Rumors

- **Mechanic:** Lot of these coming in with the same problem. Water pump. Every single one.
- **Mechanic:** Two of the shop trucks are gone. Do not ask what replaced them.
- **Mechanic:** Scrap prices went up, so somebody is stripping the wrecks out past the pass.
- **Mechanic:** Brakes are down across the board because everybody drives past here to see what happened.
- **Mechanic:** Fuel filters. That is all anybody needs now. Filters and prayers.
- **Mechanic:** Got a vehicle in the back with no paperwork and a lot of mileage. Not asking questions.
- **Mechanic:** The supplier is running late through the mountains, which means late in general.
- **Mechanic:** Half the fleet in this county is one part away from being a pile.
- **Mechanic:** Cold snaps are harder on vehicles than mud, and this year the cold has been early.
- **Mechanic:** Somebody is teaching auto repair out of a church hall. Genuinely. It is going well.

### Haggling

- **Mechanic:** Shop rate is hourly, and I do not discount the hourly.
- **Mechanic:** Parts at cost, labor at rate, and that is the only way I keep the lights on.
- **Mechanic:** I can do it cheap or I can do it right. Pick one and I will stop arguing.
- **Mechanic:** If you buy the part somewhere else, I will put it on for free. Labor still applies.
- **Mechanic:** Call ahead and I will have it ready. Do not show up and expect the miracle.
- **Mechanic:** Cash gets it out tonight. Card gets it out when the parts truck gets here.
- **Mechanic:** I will give you the old part in a bag so you can show somebody why I charged you.
- **Mechanic:** That is not a repair, that is a miracle. Miracles cost more.
- **Mechanic:** Pay the deposit or I put it back on the list, and the list is long.
- **Mechanic:** You want it cheap, drive it to the next town. I hear the guy there is giving them away.

### Goodbyes

- **Mechanic:** Watch the oil light for the first hundred miles. I mean it.
- **Mechanic:** If it makes that noise again, stop driving and call me. Do not be brave.
- **Mechanic:** Come back when it is due, not when it breaks. That is the whole tip.
- **Mechanic:** Take the tools back when you are done. All of them.
- **Mechanic:** Drive it easy for a week. One week. Then do whatever you were going to do.

## Gun Dealer And Sporting Goods

The gun counter is the most careful shop in the setting. Everything about the
conversation is a record, including the part where you are not talking about
firearms. These dealers know who bought what and they know what it is for, and
they have a long memory for people who lied once.

### Greetings

- **Dealer:** You are not on the appointment list, but the door was open so come on in.
- **Dealer:** Keep it short. I have three people after you and one of them is worse than you.
- **Dealer:** Browsing is fine. Touching is a conversation we have to finish before I answer questions.
- **Dealer:** Sit down over there. I will bring what you are here for and you can be surprised later.
- **Dealer:** You have been around before. I remember everybody who has been around before.
- **Dealer:** Sign in the book. Everybody signs in the book. Do not be the one person who does not.
- **Dealer:** I got a thing in the back that I do not keep in the case. Just so you know it exists.
- **Dealer:** If you are here to talk, we can talk. If you are here to buy, that is also talking.

### Rumors

- **Dealer:** Ordered six of a model I have not sold in a year. That is not a retail pattern.
- **Dealer:** Ammunition is rationing itself again. There is no explanation and I make none up.
- **Dealer:** Two guys from out of state cleared the hunting rifles shelf before I opened.
- **Dealer:** My supplier called and said do not order anything with a long barrel for now.
- **Dealer:** There is a man buying optics in ones, and he is polite about it, which is worse.
- **Dealer:** The federal paperwork took nine weeks and my regular is out of patience.
- **Dealer:** Half the shops in the next county are closed. Where did those customers go, I wonder.
- **Dealer:** Somebody returned a rifle that was never fired and cleaned it twice. That is a story.
- **Dealer:** Training classes are full. Full every week. That tells you what people are planning.
- **Dealer:** There is a private security outfit buying in bulk and asking about armor. Real armor.

### Haggling

- **Dealer:** Price is the price and it is the same for everybody in this room.
- **Dealer:** I do not haggle, I negotiate, and the difference is who wants it more.
- **Dealer:** I will throw in a case and a cleaning kit. That is the best I am doing today.
- **Dealer:** Pay cash and it is done in ten minutes. Paperwork means I have to know who you are for a week.
- **Dealer:** That model sells itself. This model I would have to talk you into, so I priced it accordingly.
- **Dealer:** If you are buying in quantity, talk to me before you talk to the catalog.
- **Dealer:** I will not sell you something you cannot train on. That is not a sales tactic, it is a store policy.
- **Dealer:** Come back with a real answer and I will hold the good one behind the counter.
- **Dealer:** You have been in here three times and bought nothing. I am starting to worry about you.
- **Dealer:** Trade in your old one and we will talk, but I am going to undervalue it and I will tell you by how much.

### Goodbyes

- **Dealer:** Take the manual. Reading it is not optional.
- **Dealer:** Get it registered before you do anything interesting with it.
- **Dealer:** Keep it locked when it is in the truck. Always. No exceptions, not once.
- **Dealer:** If anybody asks, I sold you a used one cheap. That is all I know.
- **Dealer:** Come back and see the new ones. Do not look at them, that starts something.

## Tavern And Shop Regional Variants

Same use, different state. Pick by section from FACTIONS.md.

### Pacific Compact

- **Bartender:** Everybody here is from somewhere else and everybody has an opinion about back home.
- **Shopkeeper:** It shipped in, so we sell it, and nobody asks where it started.
- **Mechanic:** Salt does more damage here than the mileage does. Wash the undercarriage.
- **Dealer:** There is no season on this coast. Neither does anybody's spending.

### Mountain Alliance

- **Bartender:** Off season here means the bar is empty and the weather is worse.
- **Shopkeeper:** We order once a year and spend all year explaining it.
- **Mechanic:** Altitude took something out of that engine and I am not going to guess what.
- **Dealer:** Everybody up here owns more guns than they need and needs more ammo than they bought.

### Great Lakes Union

- **Bartender:** Harvest decides how loud this room gets. Watch it in September.
- **Shopkeeper:** Anything with a motor, a bearing, or a seal. That is the whole inventory list.
- **Mechanic:** Salt roads in the winter are harder on a vehicle than any mud you will find here.
- **Dealer:** Mud season is not a season, it is an event, and everybody hunts through it.

### Southern Compact

- **Bartender:** Kitchen is the real business. Bar is where people admit what they ordered.
- **Shopkeeper:** Air conditioning parts, ceiling fans, and ice. It is that kind of store.
- **Mechanic:** Heat is the extra load. Everybody waits until it strands them.
- **Dealer:** Everybody in this county has a grandfather's gun and no idea whose it was.

### Lone Star Frontier

- **Shopkeeper:** If you need it now, it is in the back and the price reflects that.
- **Mechanic:** Out here a breakdown is a schedule change, so plan for two of everything.
- **Dealer:** Yard is unlocked. Pick it up with the gate open and we are both dummies.
- **Bartender:** Long drive in, so the first pour is a courtesy. Second one you earn.

### Atlantic Corridor

- **Bartender:** Rent is the drink price here. Same bottle, better neighborhood.
- **Shopkeeper:** Everyone here has a coupon for a store that closed.
- **Mechanic:** I can work on anything except the ones nobody will admit they own.
- **Dealer:** Permits, paperwork, and a waiting list. Yes, in this state, yes.
