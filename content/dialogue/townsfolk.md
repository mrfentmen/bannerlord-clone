# Townsfolk Ambient Dialogue

Short flavor lines that townspeople say while you are in town, at a market, a
tavern, or a siege camp. These are background texture, not quest content. No
speaker names, no delivery notes, just the line. They are grouped by section so
a town in Texas sounds like Texas and a town in Ohio sounds like Ohio, and
within each section by mood so a line can be picked to match the situation:
a happy town, a hungry town, a town with an army sitting in it.

Rules for anything added here later: plain punctuation only, no em dashes, no
fancy quotes, one thought per line, keep it under about fifteen words. Lines are
meant to be read by anyone, so do not lean on inside references or on a specific
quest.

## Pacific Compact

California, Oregon, Washington, Hawaii, Alaska. Ports, orchards, and a lot of
people who moved here from somewhere else.

### Small talk

- We get four kinds of weather here. Two of them are summer.
- That is the third fog bank this month. The marine layer never gets bored.
- Traffic on the bridge is worse than the war, and I say that as a joke.
- Half this town showed up from three different states to complain about one bridge.
- The water is the best in the state. I pay too much to live next to it and I would pay more.

### Rumors

- Word from up north is they are pulling boats off the docks before dark.
- Somebody saw a convoy go through at three in the morning, all of it covered up.
- They are saying the coastal counties are about to stop taking food from the inland.
- A ship came in empty and left full. Nobody will say what was in it.
- The military just bought every tarp in town. That is never a good sign.

### Complaints

- The fruit is fine, the shipping is not, and the middlemen are the reason.
- Our port makes more money than our farms and we still eat the farms.
- Every time the coast gets rich, the interior gets blamed for being poor.
- Water is a fight every summer and it is the same fight every summer.
- We pay for the harbor. We get the ship noise.

### Jokes

- Half of this town is from somewhere else, and the other half is angry about it.
- The city voted to fix the roads by widening them. The roads are still broken.
- A fortune teller told me my shipment would arrive on time. She charged me for that.
- Do not ask me where I was in the war. Ask me who fixed the dishwasher.

### With an army in town

- Soldiers everywhere and not one of them will help carry groceries.
- They took the good chairs off the porch. Chairs, my friend.
- I am not a spy. I am a woman with a loud voice, which is different.
- If you are recruiting, at least knock first.

## Mountain Alliance

Montana, Idaho, Wyoming, Utah, Colorado, Nevada, Arizona, New Mexico. Mining
towns, long drives, thin air.

### Small talk

- The altitude is fine. You just have to earn it for a week.
- Two hours of nothing, then a town with one bar and one traffic light.
- We do not have seasons. We have cold, less cold, and the part where it snows sideways.
- The mountains do not care about your contracts, and that is refreshing.
- My truck has more miles on it than I have money, and we both got old together.

### Rumors

- The mine opened a new shaft. Nobody has seen what came out of the last one.
- They are hiring outside crews, and not the kind that talk to their families.
- A rail load went out at night with more guards than grain.
- Two rulers on the same mountain have not spoken in nine years, and their people are shooting at each other over a road.
- There is gold up there, and the water is worse, and the two facts are related.

### Complaints

- The company pays well and owns the housing, and those two facts are not a coincidence.
- My neighbor works for the mine and my brother works the other mine and we cannot talk at holidays.
- Nobody in this state has ever been a full day from water.
- A town that size should not need a traffic circle.
- The ore decides our taxes, so the ore decides our schools, and the ore does not care.

### Jokes

- The only thing in this town with more lawyers is the mountain, and the mountain wins.
- We voted for the mine. The mine voted by making us all rich in a way we cannot spend.
- Do not ask me to name the ten biggest employers. Three of them share a mailbox.
- The trail is marked, the maps are wrong, and the local guide has a bandage on it.

### With an army in town

- An army camp is a bad neighbor. Worse than a mine strike, and louder.
- They put the camp up on the state line so it is legally nobody's fault.
- Half my neighbors are in that camp. Do not tell me your orders.
- Water is a real issue out there, but here it is just about who drinks first.

## Great Lakes Union

North and South Dakota, Nebraska, Kansas, Iowa, Minnesota, Wisconsin, Michigan,
Illinois, Indiana, Ohio, Missouri. Grain, factories, weather that does not
commit.

### Small talk

- Corn here, timber there, and a river in the middle of everything.
- The ground is flat enough to see weather coming from three counties away.
- First snow is a holiday, second snow is a nuisance, third snow is a lifestyle.
- My grandparents farmed the same quarter section I do. The rent went up, the weather did not.
- Everybody in this town has an opinion about tractors and all of them are loud.

### Rumors

- Grain is moving out of state faster than it used to and nobody is happy about the pace.
- Somebody cornered the elevator slots. Farmers are noticing, and farmers talk.
- The mills are hiring, but only the ones that can pass a background check.
- There is talk of the union sending wagons to guard the rail heads.
- A bad harvest is a rumor until the fourth week of July, and then it is a fact.

### Complaints

- We grow the food for the whole map and we eat last.
- Every side wants to buy cheap and every side wants us to keep selling.
- The levy call took my cousin and the tax on the same acreage.
- Flat land has flat wisdom. Nothing here is hidden, including our problems.
- Good harvest, bad prices. Bad harvest, worse prices. Pick one.

### Jokes

- The only thing moving faster than my combine is a rumor about a bad harvest.
- Our town fair has a Ferris wheel, a hog, and a guy who still argues about the hog.
- A committee is coming to look at our grain. Four of them will be from out of state.
- If you want a war opinion, ask two people. You will get two opposite answers and both will be loud.

### With an army in town

- A levy does not know what our fields are for, and my brother can explain it to him.
- They took the horses off the hitching posts. All of them. On the same night.
- You are welcome, but the price of feed is going up and I did not raise the price of anything.
- Two hundred men and no one knows where the latrine is. Come back after we install one.

## Southern Compact

Kentucky, Tennessee, Arkansas, Louisiana, Mississippi, Alabama, Georgia, Florida,
South Carolina, North Carolina. Long growing seasons, hot weather, and people who
hold on to what was done to them.

### Small talk

- Growing season is generous, but the summers are working against it.
- You can stand on my porch and watch a storm build all the way across the county.
- The river is the best fishing and the worst neighbor.
- We got religion, football, and a barbecue pit, and we schedule around the pit.
- It is humid enough to read a book on the outside of an air conditioner.

### Rumors

- The militia is calling up early and not telling anybody why.
- A ship came into the bay with more guns than cargo.
- Hurricanes are forecast further out than they used to, or the port authorities are.
- Two houses over, some family has been nursing a grudge since before the war and it is about a fence and a mule.
- The governor is in the capital. The old families are in the capital too. That is the problem.
- Supply trains going north are heavier than the ones coming back.

### Complaints

- Cheap soldiers are not the problem. Keeping them loyal is the problem.
- Everyone calls up the same hundred names every single time.
- The heat takes more of us than the fighting does, and it does not file paperwork.
- We lose good young men to arguments between people who will never stand in the rain again.
- The tax man comes every season and the hurricane season comes every season, and one of them talks back.

### Jokes

- Welcome to the South. It is hot, it is humid, and both of those are legally the same complaint.
- The church, the bar, and the town hall all close at the same time, and no one will say who decided.
- A neighbor who tells you what he thinks of you is saving you a long conversation.
- Our town has one traffic light, and it is broken, and everyone is fine with it somehow.
- I will fix your fence. I will also bring up 2019. Both at the same time.

### With an army in town

- A camp in the yard is a camping troop or an army, and I know which one from the truck count.
- You are eating through our garden and you are not even in here long enough to be polite.
- My daddy did this. I would rather not see the pictures again.
- Recruiting or not, leave the well alone. That is the whole of it.

## Lone Star Frontier

Texas and Oklahoma. Long roads, big ranches, and more land than people.

### Small talk

- Everything out here is measured in minutes of driving instead of miles.
- The sunsets out here make people act like they are seeing something. It is a nice lie.
- Ranch work starts before the sun and ends after, and the pay is in the same place.
- Half the county is ranches, a quarter is oil, and the rest is somebody's argument about a fence line.
- You can see weather coming for a long way out here. Nothing else, but that.

### Rumors

- There is a horse market running at night out past the county line.
- Oil money is buying up ranches nobody has looked at since the fence fell down.
- A column went out east with three days of feed and a month of supplies. Nobody is saying why.
- Border towns are quieter than usual, and the men running the feeds have new trucks.
- There is a land claim being filed in somebody's name, and everybody knows it is not really theirs.

### Complaints

- The road is state maintained, and the state is mostly maintained somewhere else.
- Big money moves in, and the small place is a store, a feed store, and a gas pump.
- Distances out here are the actual enemy. Supply is a problem you can point at.
- Neighbors are a legal concept with a fence in the middle of it.
- A dry year here is a drought everywhere else, and nobody believes us until the price moves.

### Jokes

- That is not a dust bowl, that is a thousand acres of opinion.
- A rancher, a lawyer, and a regulator walk into a bar. The bar is the only one that leaves with a profit.
- The rodeo is the only place in this county with a strict dress code and a worse parking situation.
- Do not say you can ride until you have sat on a fence for two hours listening to somebody explain it.

### With an army in town

- An army eats a whole ranch worth of feed in a week and calls it logistics.
- We do not ask what you are doing here, and we do not need to. We can count trucks.
- You are welcome, but the cattle do not understand neutral ground.
- If you need room to drill, go behind the section line. That is not a complaint, that is a map.

## Atlantic Corridor

Maine through Virginia, plus D.C. Rich, crowded, and permanently out of food.
Everyone here has a lawyer and an opinion about someone else's money.

### Small talk

- You can see six counties from one hill, and there is not one farm in the six.
- The trains are on time and the rent is not.
- Every block has a coffee place, a bank, and somebody asking about a loan.
- D.C. is a separate animal, and the locals here are careful about which hat they are wearing.
- Everything I need is eight miles away and there is no way to walk it.

### Rumors

- The banks know about the harvest situation before the farmers do.
- A bill is being drafted that would change everything, and the draft is worse than the rumor.
- Somebody paid a fortune to put a lobbyist between a governor and his own legislature.
- The port is quiet, and nobody can say why it is quiet.
- There is a fleet anchored offshore that is not on any list I have seen.
- A third of the debt in this section is owed by people who do not live here.

### Complaints

- We have the money and none of the bread. That is the whole story of this coast.
- Everyone wants the food, and everyone will not take our price.
- The taxes are city rates and the services are county sized.
- My neighbors are six different states in one building and we all voted different ways.
- I pay more in rent than a farm in the Midwest makes in a year. For a room. With a hallway.

### Jokes

- On the coast, it is not a discussion, it is a transaction with extra steps.
- The rent went up again, which is the only service in this city that never lays off anybody.
- I have seen two empires in my lifetime, and both of them raised fees.
- Every conversation here has a lobbyist in it who is not in the room.

### With an army in town

- An army of this size in a city this dense is a bad idea on every level, including moral support.
- I am happy to feed you. I would like it in writing, from somebody I can sue.
- The trains keep running, and that is the only reason this place works, and you are blocking the trains.
- If you are here to protect us, you picked a city that protects itself badly but expensively.

## Anywhere: Weather And Work

Region neutral, safe to use in any town.

### Small talk

- The generator is running, so the refrigerator works, and that is the whole economy.
- Everybody in town has the same idea and we are all wrong.
- There is one road out and we all know it, so we are polite about it.
- The dog is not mine but he sleeps here, so now he is.
- If it is clear in the morning, clear in the morning.

### Complaints

- The work is seasonal and the bills are not.
- Everyone wants a decision this week and nobody wants a plan.
- Pay is fine, housing is not, and that decides everything about who stays.
- If it takes two hours to get a load of anything out of here, somebody is going to try something stupid.

### Jokes

- The town meeting ran four hours and the important decision was about a bench.
- A good day here is a day nobody mentions being robbed, and that is a low bar that we clear most weeks.
- Two trucks will fit on that bridge if one of them is honest about its weight.
- Somebody has to be the one to say the quiet thing out loud, and this week it is me.
