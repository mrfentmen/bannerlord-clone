# RADIO.md — station recipes (task 4D)

Reproducible recipes for the three radio stations. Everything is
generated by `python3 tools/radio/run.py --ci`; this document records
the musical and technical choices so a future pass can remix or extend
them. All output is CC0, synthesized on this VM with numpy/scipy.

## Station overview

| id | name | length | format |
|---|---|---|---|
| station-street | Street Radio | 120 s | 92 BPM boom-bap, seamless loop |
| station-night | Night Shift | 120 s | dark ambient, seamless loop |
| station-talk | Corner Talk | 150 s | talk-radio texture, seamless loop |

Masters: 44.1 kHz 16-bit mono WAV in this directory, normalized to
-16 LUFS integrated (BS.1770). Delivery: 128 kbps MP3 in
`clients/campaign/public/audio/radio/`, the paths
`clients/campaign/src/audio/radio.ts` expects.

## station-street — Street Radio

46 bars at 92 BPM = exactly 120 s.

- Drums: kick (pitch-drop sine 121→43 Hz) on beats 1 and 3 with pickup
  variations per 4-bar cycle; snare (193 Hz tone + 1.4-5.2 kHz noise)
  on 2 and 4; swung 8th hats (off-beats delayed 6% of a beat); open
  hat on the last 8th of every 2nd bar.
- Bassline: Am-F-C-G roots (A1/F1/C2/G1) in 8th notes with octave pops
  on alternating bars; all pitches qfreq-quantized to integer cycles
  over 120 s.
- Stabs: Am7 Rhodes-ish chord (220/261.63/329.63/392 Hz) on the
  downbeat of every 4th bar, 6 Hz tremolo (720 cycles: integer).
- Vocal-chop accents: single formant-filtered syllables ("a"/"e"/"o"
  vowels), bandpassed 900-3600 Hz, every 8th bar — street-report
  flavor without intelligible speech.
- Vinyl crackle: 900 sparse impulses, ±0.02-0.09 gain.
- Glue: tanh soft-clip at 1.1x.

## station-night — Night Shift

- Pad: Am9-ish voicing (110/164.81/196/246.94/293.66 Hz) as detuned
  sine pairs; the detune (x1.0035) is re-quantized with qfreq so the
  beat frequency itself completes integer cycles. Slow 3-cycle swell
  over 120 s. Lowpassed at 1400 Hz with steady_filter (two-copy
  technique) so the filter startup transient cannot click at the loop
  boundary.
- Sub pulse: 55 Hz sine with 0.5 Hz pumping AM (60 cycles: integer).
- Plucks: sparse pentatonic melody (A3-C4-D4-E4-G4-A4) at fixed
  timestamps with dotted-8th echo taps (0.375 s / 0.75 s).
- Risers at 26/56/86 s: exponential sine sweep 180→1200 Hz + bandpassed
  noise, 4 s rise into a soft highpassed crash.
- Airy wash: frequency-domain periodic noise, bandpassed 300-2400 Hz
  with steady_filter, at 0.05 gain.
- Vinyl crackle: 500 sparse impulses.

## station-talk — Corner Talk

- Bed: lo-fi keys cycling Am-F-C-G (12.5 s per chord, 12 chords),
  soft sine stacks lowpassed at 2200 Hz with per-note attack/decay;
  brushed hats (6 kHz highpass) on 0.42 s grid; vinyl crackle.
- Talk: eight phrases at 4/24/44/64/86/106/126/141 s, 6-12 s each.
  Each phrase is 20-40 syllables: 24-harmonic sawtooth at ~118 Hz
  (±6% phrase arc, 5.2 Hz vibrato) through three parallel formant
  bandpass filters (adult-male F1/F2/F3 per vowel), telephone-banded
  350-3400 Hz. Abstract texture, not intelligible speech.
- Station-ID jingle at 1 s and 75 s: five-note motif
  (A4-C5-E5-G5-A5) with a 0.8 s riser.
- Mix: bed raised and voice tamed vs. the first draft, tanh at 1.9x,
  so the crest factor survives -16 LUFS normalization without
  peak-limiting.

## Replacing the talk texture with real DJ lines

The mix slot is fixed: phrases at the timestamps above, telephone band
350-3400 Hz, bed ducks ~2 dB under voice (static mix). To drop in real
voice lines, render one WAV per phrase (same timestamps/durations),
bandpass 350-3400 Hz, and mix at the current phrase gain. The
`talk_phrase()` generator in tools/radio/synth.py can then be retired.

## Regeneration

```bash
python3 tools/radio/run.py --ci
```

Deterministic: fixed seeds (401/402/403) give identical WAV bytes.
MP3 bytes depend on the local ffmpeg build; the CI gate validates
decoded audio, not hashes.
