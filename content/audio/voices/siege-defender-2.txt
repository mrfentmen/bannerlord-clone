They are at the south wall. South wall, everybody who can walk.
Keep the doors barred and the windows lit. Make them think twice.
Do not leave the well. If we lose the well we lose the week.
Wounded go to the cellar, not the street. The cellar, I said.
Hold the landing, not the stairs. Let the stairs eat them.
If the wall falls, the school is the wall. Everybody knows the plan.
Stay off the parapet and keep your rifles loaded. They are not done.
