Kneel. You know the sentence. We all heard it read.
Any last words, say them to the sky. Nobody here is writing them down.
This is not anger. Anger would be quicker. This is the law, and the law is slow.
You had your trial. You had your say. This is the part after.
Look at me when it happens. It is the least you owe the people watching.
