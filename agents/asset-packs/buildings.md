# Agent 1 — BUILDINGS: 3D Model Art Catalog

**Project:** Mount & Blade-style browser campaign game, set in modern America (real US geography)
**Target:** Low-poly, game-ready, GLB/FBX/OBJ. Budget aim: **< ~10k tris per building** for browser perf.
**Scope:** Houses, apartments, police/fire stations, shops/strip malls/gas stations, warehouses/industrial, downtown offices.
**Method:** Catalog only — no files downloaded. Sources: Kenney, Quaternius, itch.io, Sketchfab (via public API), OpenGameArt, Poly Haven.
**Date:** 2026-09-30

## License Concerns

1. **CC0 is the safe core.** All 4 Kenney city kits and all 6 Quaternius packs are CC0 — commercial-safe, no attribution required, no viral terms. Build the campaign shell on these.
2. **Sketchfab CC-BY requires visible attribution.** ~19 of the finds. Cheapest option, but ship a credits screen and keep author names handy. Also note Sketchfab "CC Attribution-NoDerivs" items exist in this space and were **excluded** (no remixing = unusable for a modular campaign).
3. **Share-Alike is the real trap.** MRowa Apartment Houses + Roads/Bridges are **CC-BY-SA**, and the OGA "Modern building" is **CC-BY-SA 3.0/4.0**. SA can be read as applying to the whole game binary. **Avoid for the main codebase** unless you accept the viral reading; prefer the CC0/CC-BY alternatives listed for the same subcategories.
4. **Unclear/no-license itch.io pages.** 38491748, Gilligan89, and Assets.fun list **no license text on their pages**. Do not use until the author confirms terms in writing — "purchased" ≠ "licensed."
5. **Custom (non-OSI) itch.io licenses are single-tier.** MCrafterzz = "anything but reupload"; Akochan = "royalty-free personal + commercial, no resell." Both fine for shipping a game, both forbid redistributing the raw assets. Keep the source files out of any public repo.
6. **AI-assisted assets.** Akochan, awlok.dev, and (per its tag) others are flagged AI-assisted on itch. Fine legally, but a shipping commercial product may draw publisher/platform pushback — worth a policy decision now.
7. **Non-US architecture.** Paris Police Station and the Asian-themed city packs need signage, livery, and storefront swaps to read as America. Budget art time for this; it is not free.

## Browser-Perf Notes

- **Draw calls beat tri count.** Akochan's shared-atlas single-mesh design is the pattern to copy — texture atlases + merged meshes let you stay under 10k tris *and* under a few hundred draw calls.
- **Fragmentation is the enemy.** Stripmall (64k), Gas station (52k), MRowa Apartments (66k) and the OGA/Kenney/Quaternius mega-packs are all single oversized files. Budget an hour per mega-pack to split into per-unit chunks with frustum culling, or ship 3 LOD tiers.
- **Use LOD aggressively.** Kenney and Quaternius are designed for scale; the Sketchfab singles are already near the ceiling.
- **Colliders are not included on Sketchfab.** Sketchfab gives you geometry only — you will author collision separately. MCrafterzz is the only find shipping prefabs with accurate + cheap collider variants.
- **glTF/GLB everywhere.** Quaternius Downtown City MegaKit and Akochan both ship GLB; Kenney conventionally does. Sketchfab gives .glb. Route everything through glTF for the web build.
