# ERA.md

How the 1950s to 2000s setting works as a game system. Status: **working assumption**, pending the era decision in CHANGELOG.md. See FEATURES.md section 1 for the tier table this expands.

---

## 1. THE CALENDAR

- The campaign runs from **1950 to 2009**. The player picks a start year, or a scenario decade.
- One campaign clock drives everything: tech unlocks, prices, disease, media, and infrastructure.
- A full playthrough can span decades, so characters age, marry, have heirs, and die (RULERS.md section 8).

## 2. TECH AS DATA

Technology is a table, not a script. Each entry says what it is, when it exists, and what a side needs to use it.

```
tech_items: id, category, name, unlock_year, min_industry, min_money, requires[], effects
```

Categories: small arms, heavy weapons, armor, vehicles, medicine, communications, media, industry, fortification, infrastructure.

**How a side gets an item:**
1. The item's `unlock_year` has passed.
2. The side meets its industrial and money requirements (`min_industry` derives from metal output and factories).
3. Or the side acquires it another way: trade, capture, or a foreign deal paid in gold.

This means a rich side adopts new tech early, a poor side adopts late, and a side can leapfrog by capturing equipment. Adoption writes shared fields (for example `medicine_tier`, `comms_tier`, `armor_tier`) that other systems read. It never triggers scripted events.

## 3. TIERS AND WHAT THEY CHANGE

| Tier | Years | Small arms | Vehicles | Medicine | Comms and media |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1950 to 1959 | Bolt-action, early semi-auto, submachine guns | Trucks, jeeps, early armor | Basic hospitals, limited antibiotics | Field phones, radio, newspapers |
| 2 | 1960 to 1979 | Assault rifles, machine guns, rocket launchers | Armored carriers, better tanks | Wider vaccines, better surgery | Radio and TV broadcast, wiretaps |
| 3 | 1980 to 1989 | Improved rifles, night vision, anti-tank missiles | Mechanized infantry | Advanced trauma care | Field radios common, early computers |
| 4 | 1990 to 2009 | Better optics and armor, precision weapons | Fast mechanized forces | Modern hospitals and drugs | GPS, mobile phones, internet |

Tier boundaries are soft. Items unlock year by year in the table, not all at once.

## 4. WHAT ERA CHANGES IN OTHER SYSTEMS

Each of these is a value read by an existing system, so cause and effect stays intact.

- **Disease:** infection rates and death rates fall as `medicine_tier` rises. Outbreak types vary by era (generated by the disease system from tier and conditions, never scripted by date).
- **Food:** yields per worker rise over decades with farming tech, so a 1990 harvest is bigger than a 1950 one.
- **Economy:** inflation, industry share, and trade patterns shift over time. Factories and fuel matter more in later tiers.
- **Politics and media:** more channels of media over time means faster shifts in loyalty and unrest (INFRASTRUCTURE_AND_MEDIA.md).
- **War:** artillery, armor, and comms change how sieges and marches work (MARCH_AND_WAR.md).
- **Fortifications:** bunkers, minefields, and fortified compounds unlock by tier.

## 5. DATA REQUIREMENTS (important)

Real data is the rule (CONSTITUTION.md section 1), so the era needs era data:

- **Historical population:** census data exists for states and cities by decade. Use the census for the chosen start year, not current numbers.
- **Historical city sizes and boundaries:** use decade census and boundary files.
- **Road network by year:** the interstate system was built over decades, so the road network changes. Sources for historical roads are patchy. Decide in Phase 0: either use the current road network everywhere (simple, less accurate) or gate major highways by opening year using available records (better, more work). Log the decision in CHANGELOG.md.
- **Industry and farmland by year:** use historical agricultural and economic statistics where available.
- If historical data for a needed field cannot be found for a year, do not invent it. Log it under Unresolved and use the nearest real decade.

## 6. FICTIONAL HISTORY RULE

- Real geography, real city names, and real historical population data are used.
- **Events are fictional.** The game never replays real wars, dates, or crises. The sides, rulers, and conflicts are invented and system-driven.
- No real people appear as characters (CONSTITUTION.md section 6).
- In-game item names should be generic or fictional (for example "bolt-action rifle" or "light utility truck") even if the 3D model resembles a real one. This avoids trademark trouble and false historical claims.

## 7. START YEAR SELECTION

The start screen shows:
- Start year or scenario decade
- The tech tier for each side in that year
- How the side ratings in FACTIONS.md change (a 1950 Great Lakes side is more industrial than a 2005 one, and so on, computed from era data)

Side ratings must be **recomputed from the chosen year's data**, never reused from a different year.
